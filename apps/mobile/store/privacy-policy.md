# Privacy Policy — Gopala (Smart Dairy & Cattle Management)

_Last updated: 2026-08-08_

Gopala ("the app", "we", "us") helps dairy farmers manage cattle records — cows,
milk production, health, breeding, and reminders. This policy explains what data
the app handles and how.

## 1. Information we collect
- **Account information.** When you sign in, we collect your **email address** and
  name via our authentication provider (Clerk). If you use "Continue with Google,"
  Google shares your basic profile (name, email) with us.
- **Farm records you enter.** Cattle details, milk/feed entries, health and
  vaccination records, heat/breeding records, calves, and reminders — including any
  **photos** of animals you add.
- **Device permissions you grant.** Camera (to scan Pashu Aadhar tags and take
  animal photos), photo library (to attach existing photos), and notifications (to
  send you reminders). These are used only for the stated features.

We do **not** collect location, contacts, or advertising identifiers, and we do not
use your data for advertising.

## 2. How we use your information
- To provide the app's features and store your records.
- To sync your data to your account so it is available across your devices.
- To send you reminders you have created or that the app derives from your records.

## 3. Where your data is stored
- Records are stored **locally on your device** and, when you are signed in, synced
  to our cloud database (Supabase) scoped to your account so only you can access it.
- If you choose **"Back up to Google Drive,"** a copy of your data is stored in the
  private app-data folder of **your own** Google Drive. We cannot access that copy.

## 4. Third-party services
- **Clerk** — authentication (sign-in, account management).
- **Supabase** — cloud database for syncing your records.
- **Google** — optional Google sign-in and optional Google Drive backup.

Each processes data only to provide these functions. We do not sell your data.

## 5. Data security
Data in transit is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS). Access to your synced records is
restricted to your authenticated account through row-level security.

## 6. Data retention and deletion
Your records are kept until you delete them or request account deletion. You can
delete individual records in the app. To delete your account and all associated
cloud data, contact us at the email below and we will remove it.

## 7. Children
Gopala is intended for adult farmers and is not directed at children under 13.

We may update this policy; the "Last updated" date will change accordingly.
