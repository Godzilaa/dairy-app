## Full description (max 4000 chars)
Gopala is a simple, farmer-first app to manage your dairy herd — built to work in
the field, even when the network doesn't.

Keep every cow's record in one place: profile and photo, breed, Pashu Aadhar tag,
parentage, milk yield, health and vaccinations, heat and breeding, calves, and your
own reminders. Everything works offline and syncs safely to your account, so your
data follows you to a new phone.

KEY FEATURES
• Cattle register — add each cow with a photo, tag number, breed and lineage.
• Milk & feed tracking — log morning and evening yield; see daily totals and a
  per-cow line graph over the month.
• Health records — vaccinations and treatments, with automatic next-due reminders
  (e.g. deworming every 3 months).
• Heat & breeding — record heat, conception and repeat-heat dates, with the
  expected calving date auto-calculated (9 months).
• Smart calendar — see heat, vaccination, treatment and expected-calving events at
  a glance, plus Amavasya (no moon) and Hunnime (full moon) days.
• Reminders — add any task with a date and time; get notified in advance.
• Works offline — record anywhere; syncs when you're back online.
• Cloud backup — your data is scoped to your account; optional backup to your own
  Google Drive.
• Multi-language — English, Hindi and Kannada.

Gopala is designed for real farms: clear screens, big touch targets, and the
numbers that matter for better milk, better care, and better productivity.

Sign in with email or Google to get started.
