Gopala — Privacy Policy

Smart dairy & livestock management · Last updated: 15 August 2026

Gopala ("the app", "we", "us") helps farmers manage cattle records — cows, milk, feed, health, breeding, and reminders. This policy explains what data the app handles and how. By using Gopala you agree to the practices described here.

Information we collect

  • Account details: when you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile identifier to create your account.
  • Farm records you enter: cattle profiles, milk and feed logs, health and vaccination records, heat/breeding data, reminders, and any photos you add.
  • Device permissions: the camera and photo library are used only when you choose to scan an animal tag or add a photo. We do not access them in the background.

How we use your information

  • To provide the app's core features and store your farm records.
  • To sync and back up your data to your account so it follows you to a new device.
  • To authenticate you and keep your account secure.

We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Where your data is stored

Your records are stored locally on your device and, when cloud backup is enabled, are synced to secure cloud infrastructure scoped to your account. Trusted service providers help us operate the app:

  • Clerk — sign-in and authentication.
  • Supabase — encrypted cloud database for syncing your records.
  • Google — Google Sign-In and optional Google Drive backup (only if you enable it).

Data retention & deletion

Uninstalling the app removes the data stored locally on your device. To delete data held in the cloud backup tied to your account, contact us at the address below and we will remove it.

Children's privacy

Gopala is intended for farm owners and workers and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected here with a new "Last updated" date.
